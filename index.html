El Círculo del Sol

"Todos somos raíz, todos somos sol"

Una experiencia botánica en la zona más icónica de Provenza, Poblado.

DANZAS ANCESTRALES Y FUTURISTAS is our rooftop's answer to Woodstock: barefoot, sun-drunk, community-first gatherings held on the Hotel 1036 Rooftop terrace every 15 days. No headliners, no hierarchy — just a rotating circle of guests who hold space differently each time: a dancer one week, a movement activator the next, a yoga facilitator or live musician after that. Some sessions lean into wellness practices, others into games and play — it varies every 15 days, and everyone is welcome: digital nomads, Medellín residents, and the simply curious.

Tickets include rooftop and pool access, one welcome drink, and a guided opening circle. Daylight hours run on vibe-coding sessions and co-working before the music takes over. Come as you are — flowers, fringe, and good energy encouraged.
